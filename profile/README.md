# Kumado

**Technology with a human touch.**

Kumado is a technology company based in Spain, building thoughtful software, AI tools and digital products. We believe the next decade of technology will not be defined by who builds the most powerful models, but by who makes them genuinely useful, trustworthy and humane for the people who rely on them every day.

## Our vision

Artificial intelligence is reshaping how we work, create and decide. We see an opportunity to turn that shift into real, lasting value: tools that give people back their time, sharpen their judgment and amplify what they are already good at, instead of adding noise or replacing the human in the loop.

We want to build a company that earns trust over the long term. That means products designed with care, honest about what they can and can't do, and built to solve real problems for individuals and teams. We favour clarity over hype, craft over speed for its own sake, and sustainable growth over vanity metrics.

## What we build

Our work sits at the intersection of **AI**, **productivity** and **digital products**. We design and ship software that connects ideas with execution, from generative tools that help people create, to workspaces that help teams think, organize and move forward together.
